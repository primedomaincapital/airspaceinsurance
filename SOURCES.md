## market-data.html — figure sources (all figures seen in public page text)

- Reports and Data, "Aviation Insurance Market" (published December 2025, modified June 2026): $5.84 billion in 2025; $9.67 billion by 2035; revenue CAGR 5.2%, 2025–2035. https://www.reportsanddata.com/report-detail/aviation-insurance-market
- Emergen Research, "Aircraft Insurance Market": $5.80 billion in 2025; $6.11 billion in 2026; $9.70 billion by 2035; CAGR 5.3%, 2026–2035; hull insurance largest share, 42.5% in 2025; war & terrorism fastest-growing line. https://www.emergenresearch.com/industry-report/aircraft-insurance-market/toc
- Atlas Magazine, "Global aviation insurance market, coverage and pricing": $4.695 billion in 2025 versus $3.759 billion in 2021 (about +25%); North America 38% of premiums; Europe 28% (about $1.31 billion); Asia-Pacific about 21%. http://www.atlas-mag.net/en/articles/global-aviation-insurance-market-coverage-and-pricing
- SkyQuest, "Drone Insurance Market": $1.75 billion in 2024; $1.9 billion in 2025; $3.82 billion by 2033; CAGR 9.1%, 2026–2033. https://www.skyquestt.com/report/drone-insurance-market
- Reports and Data, "Low Altitude Economy Insurance Market" (December 2025): $1.2 billion in 2024; $3.5 billion by 2034; CAGR 11.5%. https://www.reportsanddata.com/report-detail/low-altitude-economy-insurance-market
- The Drone Girl, "U.S. drones in 2024" (FAA data as of August 8, 2024): 785,827 drones registered; 390,027 commercial drones; 388,838 recreational pilots registered; 6,962 paper registrations. https://www.thedronegirl.com/2024/04/01/drones-in-2024/
- FAA / DHS / FBI / DoD joint statement (December 17, 2024): more than one million drones lawfully registered with the FAA in the United States. https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/dhs-fbi-faa-dod-joint-statement-ongoing-response-reported-drone-sightings
- Aerospace America (AIAA), "New entrants drive change in general aviation" (January 2, 2026): remote/drone pilot certificates 427,598 in 2024, up from 368,633 in 2023; active GA fleet 214,222 aircraft in 2023; 28.6 million GA flight hours in 2023; GA contributed $339.2 billion to the U.S. economy in 2023 and supported 1.3 million jobs (PwC study for GAMA, February 2025). https://aerospaceamerica.aiaa.org/year-in-review/new-entrants-drive-change-in-general-aviation/
- FAA, General Aviation and Part 135 Activity Survey, CY 2024: active aircraft and hours by type; summarised by General Aviation News (February 11, 2026): 213,756 active aircraft in 2024 (slight dip from 2023); more than 29 million flight hours, up about 2% from 28.43 million in 2023; piston 138,960 active aircraft (139,300 in 2023), 16.16 million hours; nearly 30,000 aircraft aged 45–49; experimental/special light sport 30,936 aircraft. https://www.faa.gov/data_research/aviation_data_statistics/general_aviation/cy2024 and https://generalaviationnews.com/2026/02/11/ga-doing-more-with-less/
- Alliance for Aviation Across America, Executive Summary (PwC study, February 2025): more than 214,000 active GA aircraft; 28.6 million flight hours in 2023; $339.2 billion contribution; 1,330,200 jobs in 2023; more than 4,400 public-use GA airports; 887,519 active pilots (FAA 2025 U.S. Civil Airmen Statistics). https://aviationacrossamerica.org/economic-impact/executive-summary/
- IATA, Global Outlook for Air Transport (June 2025): segment passengers 4,779 million in 2024, 4,988 million forecast for 2025; RPK 9,037 billion in 2024, 9,565 billion forecast for 2025; passenger traffic growth 5.8% in 2025 after 10.6% in 2024. https://www.iata.org/en/iata-repository/publications/economic-reports/global-outlook-for-air-transport-june-2025
- Airbus / Boeing 2025 deliveries and backlog, as reported January–April 2026: Airbus 793 deliveries in 2025 (766 in 2024), backlog 8,754; Boeing 600 deliveries in 2025 (348 in 2024), backlog more than 6,100 commercial airplanes; Boeing 2025 net orders 1,173–1,075 depending on accounting treatment; Boeing Commercial Market Outlook: 43,600 new aircraft through 2044 (34,830 single-aisle, 7,815 widebody, 955 freighters). https://www.americanmachinist.com/news/news/55343304/big-improvement-in-2025-jet-deliveries-orders-boeing and https://goingglobaltv.com/boeing-won-the-orders-race-in-2025-airbus-still-owns-the-bigger-advantage/
- WTW, "U.K. High Court Russia Ruling: Aviation insurance impact" (July 2025): more than 400 leased aircraft stranded; initial estimated value up to $12 billion; September 11 aviation liability losses about $2 billion; June 11, 2025 judgment covering 147 aircraft, 16 engines, insured value about $4.5 billion; settlements reducing claims to around $3 billion; operator-policy trial autumn 2026. https://www.wtwco.com/en-be/insights/2025/07/the-aviation-insurance-implications-of-the-english-high-courts-russia-leasing-ruling
- Hausfeld, "Aviation disputes legal update on the Russian aircraft lessor policy claims — lessors win": largest aviation insurance loss in history, over 400 aircraft, value in excess of $10 billion; 147 aircraft and 16 engines leased to 18 Russian airlines including Aeroflot; insured value over $4.5 billion; loss date March 10, 2022 (GR 311); AerCap $1.2 billion war-risk cap against $2.1 billion loss. https://www.hausfeld.com/en-us/what-we-think/perspectives-blogs/aviation-disputes-legal-update-on-the-russian-aircraft-lessor-policy-claims-lessors-win
- ch-aviation, "Lessors win $4.5bn Russia insurance case in UK court" (June 12, 2025): five lessors (AerCap, DAE Capital, Merx Aviation Finance, Falcon 2019-1 Aircraft 3 Limited, Genesis Leasing); AerCap 116 aircraft and 15 engines; $1 billion payout due July 2, 2025; insurers AIG, Lloyd's, Chubb, Swiss Re. https://www.ch-aviation.com/news/154900-lessors-win-45bn-russia-insurance-case-in-uk-court
- FAA Part 107 operating limits: flights capped at 400 feet above ground level, 100 mph maximum groundspeed, within visual line of sight, one aircraft per remote pilot at a time; recurrent training every 24 calendar months. https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators/become_a_drone_pilot
